Chai Empire: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026
Developer: Lost Humans Lab
Contact: losthumanslab@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Android game Chai Empire ("the app") handles. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Lost Humans Lab does not run a server that receives your data.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings and basic gameplay statistics (such as session counts and in-game milestones) are saved only on your device. They are not sent to Lost Humans Lab. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage
  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Ads may be personalised where permitted. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings under Privacy > Ads.

Consent

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your choice before ads are requested. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from the game's menu under Ad privacy choices.

Rewarded ads

Watching rewarded ads is always optional. Rewards are in-game items only and have no real-world value.

Fonts

The app loads a font from Google Fonts when online. Google receives your IP address as part of this request.

Analytics

The app's own gameplay statistics stay on your device. The app does not use a separate third-party analytics service. Google's data collection for advertising is described above.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and Lost Humans Lab does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Lost Humans Lab does not hold personal data about you, because game data stays on your device. For data processed by Google, use Google's privacy tools.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
